# Confounders
- A common cause of both treatment assignment and outcome
- Not caused by the treatment (that's a mediator)
- Not caused by both (that's a collider)
- Creates spurious association between treatment and outcome
- Ex: ice cream sales ~ shark attacks. Neither causes the other, but hot weather drives both. Adjusting for temperature kills the association.

- If item popularity is a confounder, it *will* lead to popularity bias.
