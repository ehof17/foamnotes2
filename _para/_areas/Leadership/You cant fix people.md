# You can't fix people

Trying to do so will annoy them and frustrate you

Instead, identify their strengths and build upon those.
- People like doing things they do well.

If someone has a weakness, compensate for them.

It is hardest with the people you care about most

Corollary
- Seek and you shall find
- Tend to find what we look for

If you look for best in people, you will find great people.
If you look for worst in people, you will find bad people... Probably look for their best
