# You can't control people
No matter how good of a leader u are you cannot control people

The only person you have control over is yourself

Lets say you have a strong and convincing argument and it is not adopted.
If they don't agree you can go into a ball of Hate... Treating them as dumb and subordinate... Will just get worse.

If you instead realize you can't control others, you realize that if something isn't working, you have to try another approach.

Even if others are lazy, stupid, or subordinate, you still can't change them.
Its impossible to get stuck if you keep trying others.
You wouldn't blame the code for a bug... You blame yourself.

If you can't control yourself, you can't lead or Manager

- Think before you act and consider consequence
