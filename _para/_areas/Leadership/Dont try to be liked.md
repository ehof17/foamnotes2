# Don't try to be liked - earn respect

Impossible for someone to lead without respect

Once you earn respect, it's easier to get liked.

If you focus on being liked, you can lose respect.

If someone asks for 'A', and you want to be liked and give them 'A', 'B', 'C', you aren't gaining their respect.. They may see you as someone you take advantage of.

Don't compromise, trust them to do their own job
