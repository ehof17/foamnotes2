
Identify stakeholders and give them a sense of ownership in something we are trying to accomplish

How to give someone ownership of an idea?
- Ask questions.
- Incorporate one or more of their ideas.
- Once their things have been implemented it they own it

When developing a plan, its ok to think things out in great detail. You don't need to present it in detail though, instead allow for responses and suggestions.

With the responses you get, they will fall into 3 categories.
1. Ideas you already thought of
    - Thank the contributor, now incorporate their idea into the project
2. Good ideas you haven't thought of
   - Thank them and give them credit. This is better

3. Bad ideas
   - Start a discussion, treat them with respect and ask questions. Show interest

Obstacles
- Hard to abandon your own ideas
- Learn to attack other ideas, not other People
