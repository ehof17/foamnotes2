
# How to tell if clutch is bad

1. Try to turn it manually by hand
- Most are designed to turn freely
- If it doesn't its likely the pulley wheel is seized. Need to remove + disassable compressor to tell whats wrong

2. Eletric issue
1. multimeter
    - Disengage system, multimeter set to 200 ohms

    - Clutch coil should be 2-5 ohms. Any reading outside of that range indicates bad clutch coil

2. car battery
- 2 wires connected to battery, touch the electrical contact points to the wire. 
- Should make clutch move in and out and click
- If not, clutch needs to be replaced
