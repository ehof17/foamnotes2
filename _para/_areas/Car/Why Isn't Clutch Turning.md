# Why isn't clutch turning
1. Vehicle is too hot inside 
2. Vehicle is too cold outside
3. Magnetic clutch relay is faulty. [[Clutch relay trick]]
4. Faulty cooling fan, BOTH need to work. even if 1/2 works
5. Drive belt that turns the puley belt is broke
6. Drive belt slipping on pulley wheel
    - could be cracked drive belt
    - drive belt that needs to be adjusted
7. Compressor not receiving proper voltage
8. AC doesn't have enough or have too much refridgerant
9. Clutch is simply worn out. [[How to tell if clutch is bad]]
10. Faulty [[Speed sensor]] (Lock sensor)
11. Compressor not receving voltage [[How to check if compressor getting voltage]]
12. Faulty [[pressure switch]]. Can be bypassed [[Bypassing pressure switch]]
13. Faulty Evaporator temperature sensors (Thermistor)
- Symptyom: Air coming out of vents will fluctuate
