
# Shop around for the best lender and loan product

Finding the best mortgage is important because it can save money upfront and throughout the life of the loan.

### Meet your lender
- As you work on finding the loan, a key member of your homebuying team is your lender. Your lender provides the mortgage loan that most people need to buy a home. There are many kinds of lending companies that can loan money

### Know who lenders are
Financial institutions
- Banks
- Mortgage companies
- Finance companies
- Credit unions 
- Internet lenders

Mortgage companies
- Only make mortgage loans

Finance companies
- Make many loans, including mortgage

Credit union
- Pretty competitive since they are usually local

Internet lenders
- Fast easy loans online

Government agencies
- Can find offices at  rd.usda.gov to find low to no down payment loans and low interest rate (Based on income level).

Nonprofit community development and housing organizations
- Their products are usually more affordable than financial institutions or private developers
Sellers
- private individuals, builders, private developers or manufactured home retailers
- Pitfalls from sellers: 
- Contracts for deeds (Seller can take home back if you miss only a payment or 2)
- Land contracts
- Wraparound mortgage (You pay his mortgage off)
If you are gonna do seller financing, get a lawyer and talk to housing counselor. have the property professionally appraised and inspected.

Mortgage brokers are not as common because of the bite of 87 or housing market crash some would say. They work as independent contractors between you and the lender to find the best loan available, for a fee. Look for one who is a member of National Association of Mortgage Brokers (NAMB)
