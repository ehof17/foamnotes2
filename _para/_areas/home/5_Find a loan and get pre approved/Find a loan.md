# Get pre approved before starting to look for a home
Pre approval - Apply for a loan before you actually found a house you want to buy.
Need to provide all the same information needed to qualify for the loan.
Then, you shop for something in that price range.

Pre approval is more involved than prequalification.
You apply for the loan, in advance of finding the home, and the lender agrees to give you a loan within a certain period of time under the conditions that the house you are buying is within the price limit and worth at least as much as you can borrow
A pre approval allows a more definite commitment from the lender than prequalification because it involves a thorouggh review of your financial situation.
