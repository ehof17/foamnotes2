# Down payment assistance

A DPA program is typically offered by a nonprofit organization or state or local government agency to lower-income homebuyers cover the costs of a down payment and/or closing costs. Eligibility varies depending on the program, but is typically based on income, family size, the property purchased and location.

n some communities, nonprofits or lenders offer matched savings accounts, such as individual development accounts (IDA's), which can be used toward homeownership. If you meet the eligibility criteria, these programs match funds (generally at a rate of $1 to $4) for each $1 saved up to a maximum amount.

For local DPAs, use https://downpaymentresource.com/
