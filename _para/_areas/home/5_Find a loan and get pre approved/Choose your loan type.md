
# Choose your loan type

Safest type of loan for most first time home buyers is a fixed rate mortgage

There are other types of loans available, but they have pitfalls and are generally not the best options for first-time homebuyers and/or for lower income households:
Adjustable-Rate Mortgages (ARMs)
- which start out at an interest rate below the average fixed-rate loan, and later the rate changes on a regular schedule depending on the loan program.
Balloon-Payment Mortgages
- which has fixed monthly payments based on a 30-year payment schedule, but the entire loan balance comes due sooner – usually within five, seven or 10 years.
Graduated Payment Mortgages
- where you start out making lower monthly payments, and then, over a period of years, the payments go up slowly.
Bi-weekly Mortgages
- where you make payments every two weeks instead of monthly and can possibly pay off your loan sooner and save thousands of dollars in interest.


| Loan Type | Pros | Cons | Borrower Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed-Rate Mortgage | Predictable payments | No benefits if rates fall<br>Initial rate higher than an ARM | Cautious borrowers<br>Long-term owners |
| Adjustable-Rate Mortgage | Initial low interest rate<br>Higher loan amount<br>Payment decreases when rates fall | No stability<br>Payment increases when rates rise | Short-term owners |
| Balloon-Payment Mortgage | Lower interest rate than a fixed-rate mortgage | Refinancing may be required to pay off the balloon | Short-term owners |
| Graduated-Payment Mortgage | Higher loan amount<br>Initial low payments | Risk of negative amortization or no debt reduction through amortization | Short-term owners<br>Long-term owners with expected income growth |
| Biweekly Mortgage | Smaller payments<br>Quicker repayment | More payments per year | Borrowers with sufficient cash flow |
