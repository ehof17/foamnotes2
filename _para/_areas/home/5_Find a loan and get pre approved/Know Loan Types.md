# Know loan types
Getting a mortgage is relatively easy. The complicated part is finding the right loan for you. There are 4 main categories


### Conforming Conventional Mortgages
- Make by for profit lenders, not insured by federal govt.
- Lenders generally set the requirements, rates, limits and fees for conventional loans, based on [[secondary market]] guidelines.
- If you get a conventional loan and down payment is less than 20%, You will have to purchase [[Private Mortgage Insurance]]
### Government-Insured Loans
- guaranteed or insured by federal government. Government guarantees the lender that it will pay off your loan if you can't. You still lose the house though dummy. Typically lower interest rates

#####  FHA Loans
- Downpayment normally 3.5% of purchase price. No income limits for [[FHA Insured loans]], but there is a limit to how much you can borrow. Ratios are usually 31/43% and your and your credit history is usually evaluated more leniently than for conventional loans.

##### VA Loans
- Veterans, no down payment ratios usually 40%

##### USDA Rural Development loans
- They offer below-market interest rates, require low or no down payments and have flexible qualifying guidelines, with ratios of 29/41%.

### Below Market Rate Loans
- and other special programs for first-time buyers may be offered by government, private and nonprofit agencies to make buying homes affordable for people with modest incomes

### High Cost Mortgage Loan - HOEPA
- provides certain protections for borrowers if they take out a high-cost mortgage.
