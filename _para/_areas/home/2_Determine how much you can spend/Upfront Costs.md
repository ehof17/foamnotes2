
# Upfront Costs

Paid only once: either when buying the home or at the closing.

## Downpayment
- First investment in home. Lender decides minimum you can pay, typically 5-20% of sales price. Higher this is, lower the monthly payment

## Closing Costs
- Fees associated with load and other costs, like termite inspection. Typically 2-7% of home's price.
> Not all closing costs are paid at the time of your loan closing. You pay some loan fees, like those for credit report and appraisal, ahead of time.

## Escrows and Prepaids
- Things you pay in advance that covers the initial months' premoums for taxes and insurance. Lenders use these funds to set up scrow account.
- Lender may also want you to prepay interim interest. That is interest on money you borrow, covering days before loan payments start.
- If you buy a home on the 15th and the first payment is due on the 1st, prepay the interest for the number of days between 15th and 1st

## Reserves
- Rainy day extra funds. Some lenders require reserves equal to 2 or 3 months of house payment. Even if lender doesn't require it, its still generally a good idea to save for unexpected things.
