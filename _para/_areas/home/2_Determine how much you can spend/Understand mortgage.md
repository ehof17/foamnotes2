# Understand mortgage
Unless you are Him, you need to take mortgage.
At the time you borrow money, you promise to repay the loan, signing a [[promisary note]]
A mortgage is a [[secured loan]], agreeing to put the house as the collateral.

A mortgage payment has 4 different parts
1. Principal
- The amount you have borrowed to purchase the home. This part of the mortgage payment pays off what you borrowed.
2. interest
- The amount the lender is charging you to borrow the money. It is usually a percentage of the amount that was loaned to you. With interest, you end up paying the lender back more than you originally borrowed.
3. Taxes
- The tax portion is used to pay property taxes on your home each year. The taxes are based on how much your home and land are worth.
4. Insurance
- The insurance portion is used to pay homeowners insurance each year and sometimes mortgage insurance. Your lender requires you to have homeowners insurance in case there is a fire or similar event. Your coverage will be based on the worth of your home and includes coverage for casualty and liability.

Usually, lender figures out annual taxes, and you pay 1/12 of that each month. The lender puts those funds into a separate account, then "pays" them for you when the tax and insurance bills come through

Not all mortgage loan payments include money for taxes and insurance. Check with your lender. If your monthly payments do not, you will need to save money each month to pay your tax and insurance bills when they are due each year.
