
# Ongoing Costs

Include your mortgage payment, utilities, repairs and maintenance, are the costs you will have after you buy your home.

## utilities
- Services that make house run. When you buy your home, you are responsible for paying all utilities, such as heating oil, gas, electricity, water, sewage and garbage. 
- Internet if you are not Cave Man

## Ongoing Maintenance
- Seasonal or annual tasks associated with keeping your home in good working order. When you buy a home, you need to regularly check on and service the appliances and systems, such as heating and plumbing. Be sure to set money aside for regular maintenance.  

## Repairs
- Projects that are needed when something goes wrong with your house. When you buy a home, you will be responsible for fixing things when they break. 

## Upgrades And Improvements
- optional projects that change your home and potentially add value, such as adding another bedroom or replacing an old kitchen. 
## Taxes And Insurance
- bills you have to pay each year when you don’t have an escrow account because it’s not included in your monthly mortgage payment or you’ve paid your loan off.
