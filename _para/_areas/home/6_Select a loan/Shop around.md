# Shop around
Look for lenders and loan programs
Know your preferences
- How much of a down payment do you want to make

- What total house payment (principal, interest, mortgage insurance, property taxes, homeowners insurance and homeowners association or condo fees, if applicable) is comfortable to you?

Ask the lenders if there are any unusual or special provisions

Compare interest rates for different programs

Spend time shopping
- Looking at APR is generally the single best way to compare mortgage loan costs

Get at least three offers
- in writing. Ask each lender to give you a Loan Estimate, which is a standard form showing important facts about the loan

Review your Loan Estimates and compare total loan costs
