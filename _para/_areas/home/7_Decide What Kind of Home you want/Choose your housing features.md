# Choose your housing features
- What do you want in a house?
- How many people will live in the house?
- What activities do your family like to do?
- Do you have pets
- Do you need lots of privacy from neigbors within the home

Refer to a homebuyers worksheet as a checkoff list of things that may be important to you https://app.ehomeamerica.org/images/Homebuyers%20Wish%20List.pdf

Ask yourself: What are the top 3 housing features you can't live without?
