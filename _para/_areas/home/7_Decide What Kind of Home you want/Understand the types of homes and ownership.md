# Understand The Types Of Homes And Ownership

### detached single-family home
- stand alone home
- It is surrounded on all four sides by land and designed for one family.
- When you buy a single-family home, you own the house and the land on which it sits.
- You can make all the decisions about how you use the house and the land. This absolute ownership is called fee simple ownership.

### manufactured home 
- built in a dedicated factory, engineered and constructed to specifications set by U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development’s federal building code

### modular home
- built in a factory, but is constructed to a local or state building code


In addition to different home types, you can own them differently. Individual, with other family members, or as part of a group. Owning is a home is also called having the title.

There are 3 common ways to own a home
1. Sole and separate.
    - One person owns it, common form for unmarried people

2. Tenancy In Common
    - 2 or more people own the same property. Can be 50/50 or a different split. Individual owners can sell their part of the ownership to someone else

3. Joint Tenancy (with right of survivorship)
    - All co owners must take title at the same time and own equal parts. If a co owner dies, the other co owner ends up owning the entire property

Some states have community property laws that automatically give people in a marriage an equal share of the property. If you are not married now but want to be someday, talk to an attorney to see what will happen to your home's title after you get married
