# Find the right neighborhood.

Neighborhoods impact quality of life and future resale value. When evaluating neighborhood qualities, consider

- General safety and desirability
- Condition of other homes in the area
- Quality of local schools
- Distance from your job // commute time
- Availability of public transportation
- Recreational activities, like parks
- Easy to access shopping
- Proximity to places of worship

Get to know the feel of different neigborhoods.
- Actual walk around rather than google maps 
- Do buildings look well maintained?
- Are there other new homeowners in the neighborhood?
- Go back at night. If you are uncomfortable looking at homes in that area at night, that is a red flag.

Get crime statistics

Visit local schools during the day to see how they are run. Even if you don't have school age kids, schools impact the value of your home when you want to sell *

Find out about property tax rates online, by phone, in person.

Find out about insurance rates for homes and cars by contacting an insurance agent

If you are at a fixer upper, Ask your lender or Housing Counselor about the FHA 203(k) Loan Program that will help you find that hidden gem and finance the home and repairs into one mortgage.
More info can be found at hud.gov/program_offices/housing/sfh/203k/203k--df
