
# Advantages and Disadvantages of buying a hom

## Advantages
- Tax Benefits, all of the interest on your home loan and all of the property taxes you pay each year can be deducted from your taxable income
- Home equity
- Flexibility
- Stable housing costs if its a fixed mortgage

## Disadvantages
- No guarantee it goes up in value
- Need to take care of Repairs
- Time consuming 
- Monthly cost typically higher
- Decreased Mobility
- Fewer amenities: pools, gym
